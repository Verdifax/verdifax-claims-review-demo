# Why teams choose Quillstone Vault

Quillstone Vault stores your signed contracts with a tamper-proof seal. Once a contract is sealed, it is 100% secure: no one, not even us, can change it without the change being visible.

Each seal records the time of signing and the account that signed. You can download the sealed file and the seal together at any time.

Quillstone Vault does not review the contents of a contract and does not give legal advice.
