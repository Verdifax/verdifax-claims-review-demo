# Harborlane Match: how it works

Harborlane Match uses our patented matching method to pair bank lines with ledger entries. The method scores each candidate pair and keeps the highest score above a threshold you set.

Pairs below the threshold are listed for a person to decide. The tool does not post entries and does not change your ledger.

A patent application for the method was filed in March 2026 and is pending examination.
